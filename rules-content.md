The Ember League is a community-run League of Legends competition that aims to deliver a structured, fair, and exciting competitive experience. This rulebook applies to all participating teams, players, coaches, and representatives, and governs all operations for the current Ember League season. The Ember League is not affiliated with or endorsed by Riot Games.

All official communication, coordination, and dispute resolution will be handled via the Ember League Discord server: [https://discord.gg/UFJkczU89X](https://discord.gg/UFJkczU89X)

The League Admins reserve the right to act in the best interests of competitive integrity and may issue penalties or decisions outside the bounds of this document if necessary. Lack of verbiage in this document does not constrain the League Admins and they may use any punitive actions at their disposal to act in the best interests of the League.

## 1.2 Forfeits

### 1.2.1 Definition of a Forfeit

**A forfeit occurs when a team is unable or unwilling to play a scheduled match, resulting in an automatic loss for the forfeiting team.**

### 1.2.2 Conditions for a Forfeit

A team may be considered to have forfeited under the following conditions:

- **No Show**: Failure to have at least the minimum number of eligible players in the lobby within 15 minutes of the scheduled match time. (See Section 6.4)
- **Inability to Field a Team**: Inability to meet the roster requirements due to insufficient players or a lack of substitutes.
- **Refusal to Play**: Explicitly refusing to play in written or verbal communication regardless of the reasoning.
- **Technical Inability**: Persistent technical issues that prevent a team from starting or continuing a match after all reasonable troubleshooting efforts have been exhausted.
- **Disqualification or Withdrawal**: Any team disqualified, withdrawn from, or otherwise removed from the tournament will automatically forfeit any and all future scheduled games in the tournament.

### 1.2.3 Forfeit Policy

To maintain the competitive integrity of the Ember League, the following policies apply:

- **First Forfeit Warning**: A team's first forfeit will result in a formal warning and a review of their status within the league.
- **Multiple Forfeits**: Two forfeits within a regular season may result in the team being removed from the league and disqualified from winnings, pending review by Ember League officials.
- **Playoff Forfeits**: Any team that forfeits during the playoffs will be immediately removed from the league and disqualified from winnings, and their opponent will advance to the next round.

### 1.2.4 Forfeit Communication

Teams anticipating a forfeit must notify Ember League Officials and their opponents as soon as they believe a forfeit may be necessary.

### 1.2.5 Exceptional Circumstances

In cases of emergencies or unavoidable circumstances, Ember League Officials may waive or modify forfeit penalties at their discretion. Appeals must be submitted within 24 hours of the forfeited match.

## 1.3 Loss of Deposit

**The deposit provided upon sign-up is "in good faith" and will be returned as long as they follow these requirements:**

- Finish out the season. If the team were to forfeit in its final week, the deposit would be lost.
- No major rules were violated.

- Teams that withdraw before Week 2 will be refunded their deposit.
- Withdrawals after Week 2 forfeit the deposit.
- If a team forfeits 2 individual games or one full Best of 3 series, a $5 deduction is placed on the deposit.
- If the team forfeits more than 4 individual games or 2 full Best of 3's, the entire deposit is lost.

In the event of a team withdrawal, Admins will attempt to replace the withdrawn team with a waitlisted team, if possible.

# Section 2 - Eligibility

## 2.1 Rank Restrictions

Players are only eligible if their highest peak rank falls **within** the Original Cap of their division based on the most recent three ranked seasons: **The current season (Season 16), Season 15 (S2025), and Season 14 Split 3 (S2024 S3)**.

### 2.1.1 Eligibility Cap

- **Magma League**: Masters 100 LP or lower
- **Scorch League**: Diamond 4 99 LP or lower
- **Blaze League**: Emerald 4 99 LP or lower
- **Cinder League**: Platinum 4 99 LP or lower

Players may **climb beyond the Original Cap** after being added to the roster, but if they exceed the **Absolute Growth Cap**, they will be removed from the roster without exception.

### 2.1.2 Absolute Cap

- **Magma Division**: Players are removed if they hit above **Masters 300 LP**
- **Scorch Division**: Players are removed if they hit **Master 0 LP**
- **Blaze Division**: Players are removed if they hit **Diamond 4 0 LP**
- **Cinder Division**: Players are removed if they hit **Emerald 4 0 LP**

Absolute cap **will not apply once playoffs begin**. Players will be allowed to freely climb once the regular season is over and the rank they reach will apply to the next split.

### 2.1.3 Historic Eligibility

Players with a peak rank higher than the current division cap in past seasons may be deemed eligible to play, pending approval by an admin.

**Magma League**
- Masters 101 LP Peak (S2025)
- Masters 200 LP Peak (S2024 S3)
- Masters 250 LP Peak (Any split before S2024 S3)
- Masters 300 LP is the Absolute Growth Cap for this division.

**Scorch League**
- D3 0 LP Peak (S2025)
- D2 0 LP Peak (S2024 S3)
- Masters 0 LP Peak (Any split before S2024 S3)
- D1 99 LP is the Absolute Growth Cap for this division.

**Blaze League**
- E3 0 LP Peak (S2025)
- E2 0 LP Peak (S2024 S3)
- D3 0 LP Peak (Any split before S2024 S3)

**Cinder League**
- P3 0 LP Peak (S2025)
- P2 0 LP Peak (S2024 S3)

> **Note:** If the majority (3 of 5 players) of any given team were to exceed the Original Cap of their league by more than 2 divisions, they will be subject to an in-depth review. If the vast majority (4 or more players) exceed the Original Cap of their league by more than 2 divisions, they will be automatically disqualified. This is to avoid rank sitting pre-split as much as possible. This applies to the regular season only.

### 2.1.4 "Volcanic" Player Status

A player can be given Volcanic status in 1 of 2 ways: competitive performance in Ember and other Amateur Leagues, or exceeding Historic Eligibility. A team may not have more than **ONE (1)** Volcanic Player in any given game (playing), and **TWO (2)** total on their roster. The list of Volcanic players is a living list, and players can be added or removed at any time, but typically, players are added in the off-season. A list of all Volcanic players will be maintained by the League Admins and updated at the end of each split/upon the sign-up of a Volcanic player.

**Rank Eligibility**

Players who exceed their Ember League's **Historic Eligibility (2.1.3)** in previous splits may be permitted to join with a team as a **Volcanic Player.** A player may exceed Historic Eligibility by **ONE (1)** division for any or all of the given splits and be accepted as a Volcanic Player. For any split before the Historic Eligibility, they may have gone up to their Ember League's **Absolute Cap** and still be accepted as Volcanic. This also applies to the current season. Anyone who is within 100 LP of the cap of their league may be accepted as a Volcanic Player as well.

**Competitive Performance**

At the end of each split, the League Staff will get together and determine any players who were outliers as Volcanic based purely on in-game performance in Ember and any other Amateur Competitive League. This is an opinion-based decision made by the League Admin of each League and verified by the Commissioner to avoid favoritism or targeting.

# Section 4 - Season Format

## 4.1 Group Draw Show

The **Group Draw Show** will be held to determine the composition of the league groups. It ensures a fair and transparent process for assigning teams to their respective groups.

### 4.1.1 Draw Show Procedure

**Seeding Process:**

Teams will be seeded based on relevant factors such as **previous performance** or **average rank,** if applicable. Teams will be divided into **Seed Pools** (e.g., Pool 1, Pool 2) to ensure competitive balance.

**Draw Format:**

Teams will be randomly drawn from each pool and assigned to groups in an alternating fashion via a spinning wheel. No group shall have more than one team from the same organization.

**Broadcast:**

The draw will be **publicly broadcast** on league channels for transparency. Casters or league officials will explain the draw process live.

**Tiebreaker Adjustment:**

If an issue arises during the draw (e.g., conflicting teams), the league reserves the right to **redraw** from the affected pool.

**No Appeals:**

Teams may not appeal the results of the draw. However, any administrative errors identified during the show will be addressed by league officials.

## 4.2 Regular Season Format

Matches occur on the listed days for each league **at 8 PM ET** unless both teams agree to a reschedule (see section 7.1).

**The regular season length will be determined by the number of teams in each group, and all regular season games will be Best of 3 Fearless.**

All teams will compete in scheduled matches within their assigned group. Each match is played as a **Best of 3** series. Each team will play one Bo3 series per week over the course of the group stage, sometimes having a bye week or cross-division week (depending on the makeup of the League) that will not count towards the team's record.

If there are 8 or more teams in any given League or Division, only the **top 6 teams will advance to the playoffs.** All other teams will be eliminated from the tournament. If a League or Division has 12 or more teams, **the top 8 teams will advance to playoffs instead of 6**.

## 4.3 Playoff Format

All playoff matches will be played as **Best of 5 Fearless** and Single Elimination format. Seeding for the playoff bracket is determined by the results of the Group stage. Depending on the number of teams in the playoff bracket, 8 teams will make the playoffs. If there are 8 or fewer teams in the league, some top seeds may receive byes.

## 4.4 Tiebreaker Criteria

When two teams are tied in Match Record with each other, and the tie needs to be broken, the following criteria are used to break the tie, **IN ORDER**:

1. **Head-to-Head Record** -- If two teams are tied in Match Record, the team that beat the other team in the regular season will be the winner of the tie.
2. **Game Win/Loss Differential** -- The team with the higher individual game win percentage will be the winner of the tie.
3. **Total Game Wins** -- The team with the highest number of individual game wins will be the winner of the tie.
4. **Fastest Average Win Time** -- If still tied, the team with the shortest average win duration will be the winner of the tie.
5. **Tiebreaker Match** -- If necessary, a **Bo1 tiebreaker match** will be played.

## 4.5 Mid-Season Patch Policy

Newly released or reworked champions will be **automatically banned** for the next patch following their release before they are allowed to be played in the league. Additional information on any active champion bans will be posted in the #disabled-champs channel.

# Section 5 - Roster Management

## 5.1 Roster Limits and Changes

**Team member limitations:**
- **Minimum**: 5 players
- **Maximum**: 10 players (5 starters, 5 substitutes)

Teams may add players before the **roster lock deadline (24 hours) before a match.**

Teams may make up to three (3) roster changes per week, which includes player additions and position swaps.

The final roster lock for the regular season will be the day before the final game of the regular season. After this day, no roster changes may be made, and any other roster change beyond this point is classified as use of an **E-Sub.** (See Section 5.3)

**Solo Queue Mandate (Playoffs):**

**Players must play a minimum of 3 Solo/Duo Queue Games every week to remain eligible to play.** Any player in violation of this will be counted as an E-sub and will receive E-sub penalties. This requirement is waived once the playoffs begin.

**Substitute Players:**

Substitute Players are defined as players who are on a team's roster, but are not listed as a starter player. Teams may use a maximum of three (3) substitutes in a single match without penalty. Use of additional substitutes beyond this limit results in 2 bans lost for every game above 3 subs. Any substitute player added to a roster must be under the Original Cap of the League to which they are being added. Once they are approved, they may grow to the Absolute Cap.

**NOTICE: Substitute players are NOT E-subs. For info on E-subs, see section 5.3.**

No new roster additions are allowed once the **playoffs begin**.

## 5.2 Coaches

### 5.2.1 Coach Eligibility

- Each team may designate up to 2 coaches for their team. Coaches must be added prior to roster lock at the end of the regular season.
- Coaches **do not count toward the player rank cap** (a coach may be a higher rank than permitted for players in that division).

### 5.2.2 Coach Role and Access

- Coaches may join team communications before/between games to assist with drafts, but once the game's loading screen commences, they **must leave the call.**
- Coaches **may not participate** as a substitute player unless they are added to the roster as any other substitute would be. If they are not added 24 hours before the match occurs, they would be subject to E-Sub vetting and E-Sub penalties.

> **Note:** Substitutes on the team's roster may join the call for the same time period as coaches in between games, but must leave at the same time.

### 5.2.3 Conduct and Responsibilities

- Coaches must uphold the same standards of behavior as players.
- Toxicity, harassment, or unsportsmanlike behavior from coaches will result in the same penalties for the team as if a player had committed the rule break.
- Coaches are encouraged to assist with **team scheduling, communication, and improvement**.
- Coaches may represent the team in **official league communications** if designated as a Team Captain or the Team Owner.

## 5.3 Emergency Substitute (E-Sub) Rule

**Definition:**

An Emergency Substitute (E-Sub) is a player who is not part of the team's active roster or registered substitute list but is temporarily allowed to participate in a match due to unforeseen circumstances (e.g., last-minute absence, technical issues).

**Usage Limits:**
- An E-Sub may only be used **with prior approval** from league administrators.
- If **three (3) or more** E-Subs are attempted to be used in the same game, **that team will forfeit the match automatically.**
- **Only 1 E-Sub will be allowed per team per game during the playoffs.**

**Eligibility:**
- The E-Sub **must** meet the League they are being used in's Original Cap restrictions as listed in 2.1.1.
- E-Subs **are not** subject to the Absolute Cap afforded to regular rostered players and must currently meet their division's qualification requirements.
- The E-Sub **must not** be on another roster within the same division.
- The E-Sub **must not** have been on any roster that was disqualified or eliminated during the current season.

**Procedure:**
1. The team must notify league staff **at least 1 hour before the match start time** (exceptions may be made for emergencies). Staff must be notified via the Ticketing system or in the League's respective Roster Changes text channel.
2. The E-Sub must be added to the team's Discord channel, and the summoner name must be submitted for verification.
3. League staff will confirm or deny eligibility before the match starts.
4. The usage of E-Subs results in **two ban losses for each E-sub used**.
   - 1 E-sub = 2 Bans Lost in First Ban Phase
   - 2 E-subs = 4 Bans Lost (Three in First Ban Phase, One in Second Ban Phase)

**Penalties:**

Unauthorized use of an E-Sub may result in **match forfeiture**, **loss of deposit**, or **further disciplinary action**.

## 5.4 Roster Recycling Restriction

To preserve the competitive integrity of the league, the following restriction applies to **recently dropped, removed, or transferred players**:

1. A player **cannot be added to any other team** within the league **for a minimum of 7 days** after being **dropped or removed** from a team.
2. This includes **all teams** and applies regardless of whether the player was officially active in matches.
3. If a player is removed from **Team A** and added to **Team B**, the 7-day cooldown period begins **immediately upon the roster move being confirmed** by league officials.
4. During the cooldown period, the player **may be rostered** on the new team but **is not eligible to participate in official matches** until the cooldown has expired.
5. Any team found attempting to circumvent this rule (e.g., pre-arranged swaps or temporary drops) may face penalties including **reversal of roster changes**, **match forfeits**, or **disqualification**.

### 5.4.1 Sister Teams

To maintain competitive integrity and prevent roster manipulation between affiliated (sister) teams, the **Roster Recycling Restriction applies to all sister teams.** Even if two teams are in the same Organization (e.g., Definitely Not CB and Might Be CB both being under the CB flag), the cooldown will still apply for that swap.

# Section 6 - Game Conduct

## 6.1 Gentlemen's Agreements

All matches must be played in accordance with official Ember League rules.

Informal agreements, both verbal or in writing, between teams (aka "gentlemen's agreements") will NOT be recognized by Ember Esports staff. Any other deviations from the rules without prior admin approval will result in both teams receiving a loss for that match, regardless of the outcome, if it is during the regular season. If this is a Playoff game, the match will be replayed.

## 6.2 Voice Communication

Players must use designated **Ember League Discord voice channels**.

Only the five **playing members** may remain in the voice channel once the loading screen commences.

## 6.3 Competitive Integrity

All players are expected to abide by Discord TOS and Riot Games Summoners Code while competing in the tournament.

**Cheating, scripting, or exploiting** results in immediate disqualification.

**Sportsmanship violations** (e.g., excessive toxicity) may result in **ban losses or match forfeits**.

**No excessive BM:** All chatting must be limited to GG and minor communication with no ill intent.

## 6.4 Penalties for No-Show

Teams must be ready **within 5 minutes of match time.** A team is defined as ready when all 5 players are both in their designated team Ember Esports Discord voice call and in the League client game lobby. Any team that does not meet these requirements within 5 minutes of the match start time is considered late.

- If a team is **10 minutes late**, the offending team loses **1 phase 1 ban**.
- If a team is **15 minutes late**, the offending team loses **2 phase 1 bans**.
- If a team is **30 minutes late**, the offending team will **automatically forfeit Game 1 of the series.**
- If a team is **45 minutes late**, the offending team will **automatically forfeit Game 2 of the series, triggering an automatic loss of any Bo3 series.**
- If a team is **more than 45 minutes late**, the offending team will **automatically forfeit Game 3 of the series, triggering an automatic loss in any Bo5 series.**

**Teams that forfeit any game or series due to tardiness will not be permitted to reschedule that game or series unless explicitly approved by the Ember League Admins.** Excessive and/or repeated Forfeits will result in removal from the League and additional penalties (see Section 8.2).

## 6.5 Side Selection, Drafting, and Pre Game

Tournament codes must be used for every match. In the case that a tournament code does not work, inform League Staff, and a decision will be made on how to move forward. All teams must be present in their designated Ember League Discord Voice Channel and have their starting lineup ready in the pre-game lobby at the scheduled match time. Teams should ensure that players are lined up correctly in LCS order before starting the third-party draft tool. Players must be arranged in the League of Legends client from top to bottom in the following order: Top, Jungle, Mid, Bottom, Support.

### 6.5.1 Side Selection

Side selection will be decided via a 1v1 or 2v2 between the two teams at the designated series start time, with the winner earning **Side Selection Advantage (SSA)**. If both Team Captains agree, the 1v1 may be scheduled to a time before the series is set to begin. If either one of the teams does not wish to participate in the 1v1, an Ember League Staff Member will conduct a coin flip instead. Both Team Captains will join the Ember League Staff Member in the Support Help Desk and will have the coin flip streamed to them. These are the only two ways to determine **Side Selection Advantage**. Loser gets SSA for all games after Game 1.

**Side Selection Advantage** is defined as the opportunity to select between playing on a side of your choice, Red or Blue, or getting the first pick in the draft. If a Team Captain chooses Blue side, the opposing team will get the first pick and the Red side. If a Team Captain chooses First Pick, the opposing Team Captain gets to pick what side they would like to play.

### 6.5.2 Side Selection 1v1/2v2

The side selection 1v1/2v2 will be the default way Side Selection Advantage is determined. **For all streamed games, teams will NOT be able to opt out of this 1v1/2v2; it will be required.** All 1v1/2v2's will be played on Howling Abyss and will be blind pick. The three win conditions are first blood (or first to 2 kills in a 2v2), first tower, or first to 100 farm. There will be a time limit of 10 minutes placed on the 1v1/2v2; once you hit 10 minutes in game, the player with more farm wins. No mirror matches are allowed to be organized per 6.1 **Gentleman's Agreement.** Those found handshaking a mirror match will have their 1v1/2v2 voided and will be subject to a coin toss. There are no Summoner Spell or item restrictions. This 1v1/2v2 may be streamed to your team in your team channel, but all players/coaches must be muted to avoid coaching. It is the responsibility of the opposing teams to ensure this is followed, and if evidence is presented that this rule is broken, the offending team receives an auto-loss of SSA and the 1v1/2v2.

### 6.5.3 Drafting

Drafting should be handled with an external drafting site. Incorrect bans or picks on both the external drafting site and in the league Client could result in **penalties, at the staff's discretion**. Once you are conducting in-client drafting, each player **MUST** pick the champion they are playing; no trades are permitted within the League client.

### 6.5.4 Role Swap Rule

To ensure scouting integrity, **players must draft with the intent of playing the roles declared on their team roster and may not swap roles with teammates without submitting an official roster change at least** 24 hours prior to their game. (ex. Someone who is declared as a Jungler on their roster may not swap roles with their Support strategically during draft.) If you are listed as a top laner, you must load in with the Top Lane Quest, etc.

**Once loaded into the game, players may freely swap lanes** and adjust their gameplay based on matchups and game state. The exception to this rule is any swapping between ADC and Support, which is allowed.

## 6.6 Pauses & Disconnects

**Acceptable Reasons to Pause**

1. Technical issues (hardware malfunctions, game crashes, internet disconnections).
2. Verified client/server issues.
3. Player disconnect.
4. Emergency situations (within reason).

If a player is deemed unable to continue with a game BEFORE 10 seconds into a game, the team with the technical issue may either continue with a 4v5 in hopes the player returns or use another rostered player in the same draft. Abuse of this rule will result in an immediate forfeiture of the series.

Each team is allowed **3 pause uses per game and a total pause time of 10 minutes per game** to resolve issues listed above. Once either the total 10 minutes of pause time OR 3 total uses of pauses are used, players can no longer pause and must play the game, or they will receive penalties.

Teams are not to pause during any combat. Combat is classified as any champion taking damage from another enemy champion in the last 3 seconds. Pausing in this window should result in an immediate unpause. If the pause continues, the pausing party will receive the following penalties:

- First offense: **Warning**
- Second offense: **Loss of pause privileges**
- Third offense: **2 Ban loss for 1 game**

## 6.7 Disabled Champion Skins

Certain champion skins will not be allowed as they can cause visual clarity issues, such as making certain abilities hard to see or showing a misleading hitbox. Teams that notice the use of a forbidden skin can request a remake of the game to an admin within the first minute. If the skin is used again in the remake, the team with the violation will face penalties. (See Section 8.2)

**Banned Skins:**

Mecha Aatrox, Blackfrost Anivia, Project Ashe, Pulsefire Blitzcrank, True Damage Ekko, Arcade Ezreal, Pulsefire Ezreal, Vandal Gragas, Dragon Trainer Heimerdinger, FNATIC Jarvan IV, Forsaken Jayce, Dark Star Karma, Winter Wonder Karma, Elementalist Lux, Challenger Nidalee, Headhunter Nidalee, Nunu & Willump Bot, Zombie Nunu & Willump, Boneclaw Shyvana, Darkflame Shyvana, Arcade Sona, Atlantean Syndra, Arclight Varus, Dark Waters Vladimir, Night Bringer Vladimir.

# Section 7 - Scheduling

## 7.1 Streaming and Match Day

**Two teams** will be designated to play on the official **Match Day** each week.

These matches will be **streamed** and showcased on the league's official broadcast channels. Teams selected for broadcast will be notified at least **5 days in advance**.

**Flexible Scheduling:**

All other group stage matches for the week may be played at any time, as long as they are completed **before Friday** (Subject to change). Teams are responsible for coordinating and agreeing upon a match time. Match results must be reported immediately after completion.

**Failure to Schedule:**

If teams fail to agree on a rescheduled match time, the match defaults back to 8 PM ET on the original day. Failure to show up at the assigned time may result in an **automatic game and/or series forfeit. (See Section 6.4)**.

**Rescheduling:**

Broadcast matches may only be rescheduled under **exceptional circumstances** and require league approval. Non-broadcast matches can be rescheduled, provided both teams agree, and the match is completed before the deadline. League admin **must** be notified of **any** rescheduled match.

## 7.2 Holidays

**Scheduling Adjustments:**

Matches will not be scheduled on the following recognized major holidays. If they land on one, that week will be designated a break week for that league.

- **New Year's Day**
- **Independence Day (U.S.)**
- **Thanksgiving Day (U.S.)**
- **Christmas Eve**
- **Christmas Day**

Regional holidays may also be considered based on the majority of participating teams.

**Rescheduling Requests:**

Teams may request a reschedule if a match is initially scheduled on a holiday that Ember Esports does not already recognize. Requests must be submitted at least 7 days in advance. The opposing team and league officials must approve the reschedule.

**League Discretion:**

The league reserves the right to adjust schedules around holidays to ensure fair rest periods and competitive integrity. Matches may be shifted to accommodate travel, family commitments, or other holiday-related concerns.

# Section 8 - Penalties and Appeals

## 8.1 Penalty Type Definitions

- **Warning**: Issued for minor, non-malicious infractions
- **Ban Loss**: Loss of champion ban(s) during the draft phase
- **Player Suspension**: Player cannot compete in one or more matches
- **Game Forfeit**: One game of a match is automatically lost
- **Match (aka Series) Forfeit**: Entire match is forfeited
- **Removal from League**: Player or team is disqualified from the season

## 8.2 Penalty Glossary

| Infraction | Penalty |
|---|---|
| Emergency Substitutes | 2 Ban loss per E-Sub |
| Tournament Code not used | 1 Ban loss |
| Late Arrival | See section 6.4 |
| Not in voice channel during game | 2 Ban losses for next game |
| Pause after 10 mins or 3 pauses | Forfeit game |
| Weekly games not played | 1st week: 1 Ban loss / 2nd+ week: Player suspended for 1 week |
| Bad Pause | 2 Ban loss |
| Cheating, scripting, bug exploit | Removal from the League and banned |
| Forfeiting match | 1st: Warning / 2nd: $5 loss from deposit / 3rd: Full deposit loss / 4th+: Team removal from tournament |
| Use of disabled skins | 1 Ban loss |
| Undisclosed Summoner Name Change | 2 Ban loss |
| Failure to report Match Results | 1st: Warning / 2nd+: 1 Ban loss |
| Manipulated Match Results Reported | Match Forfeit |
| Account sharing | Player is removed from the League and banned* |
| Smurfing | Player is removed from the League and banned* |
| Discord TOS violation | (1 - 2) Ban loss / Match Forfeit |
| Elo Integrity | Player suspension |

***Ember Esports has a Zero Tolerance policy in place for Smurfing/Account Sharing.** Any team caught with players engaging in smurfing, account sharing, or other deliberate attempts to gain an unfair advantage will be subject to immediate removal from the tournament and forfeiture of any winnings, regardless of the number of players involved.

## 8.3 Reporting and Ticketing

If a rulebook violation is suspected, players/teams may report it to Ember staff via the Ticketing system.

**For No-Shows and Tardies:**
If an on-call staff member is available, the team captain making the report should join the #Support-help-desk Voice Call for immediate assistance.

If on-call staff is unavailable, the team captain should submit a ticket to the #tickets channel immediately. This submission must include a screenshot and/or a screen recording showing both the League Client Lobby and the current time. Staff will respond to the ticket as soon as possible.

**For All Other Issues:**
Tickets should be submitted promptly following a match, and will be answered by staff within 24-48 hours. Include as much detail as possible (including screenshots/screen recordings, if possible) to ensure a quick case resolution.

For further instructions on ticketing, please see the #tickets channel in the Ember Esports Discord.

## 8.4 Appeals Process

Teams or players may appeal penalties by opening a ticket in the official Discord within **48 hours** of the ruling. Appeals should include:

- Description of the event or decision
- Screenshots, Screen Recordings, or other evidence
- Reasoning for reversal or reduction of the penalty

The Board will review appeals and respond within a reasonable timeframe. All decisions are final unless new evidence is provided.

There will be a **Penalty Tracking Document** to record all offenses.
